# Power_BI_-AdventureWorks-Sales-Performance-Dashboard
Designed an interactive Power BI dashboard for AdventureWorks, analyzing 25K+ orders across $24M+ revenue. Performed data cleaning, transformation, and modeled a star schema with Sales, Customer, Product, and Calendar tables.


**Dashboard Design Overview**


**KPI Highlights:**
Total Revenue: $24.91M    
Total Profit: $10M    
Total Orders: 25K    
Return Rate: 2.17%    

**Time-Based Analysis:**
Revenue trends from Jan 2020 to Jan 2022   
Weekly orders by year   
Monthly revenue, profit, and order performance against targets   

**Product Analysis:**
Orders by Product Category    
Top 10 product sales and return rates    
Price adjustments, order vs target performance for selected products    

**Customer Analysis:**

Total unique customers: 17K     
Average Revenue per Customer: $1,431     
Orders segmented by income level and occupation     
Top 30 customers ranked by total revenue
